The Studio

Interiors that feel
like you

Lily Allspaugh Design is a full-service interior design studio guided by the belief that the most beautiful rooms are the ones that feel quietly, completely inhabited.

Working primarily in residential interiors, Lily brings an editorial eye and a curator's patience — sourcing pieces with history, texture, and weight. No room feels finished until it feels lived in.

— Lily

What We Offer

Services

01

Full-Service Design

From concept to installation — space planning, material selection, custom furnishings, and final styling. A complete transformation with your vision at its center.

02

Space Planning

Thoughtful layouts that optimize flow, function, and beauty. Whether a single room or a complete home, we bring structure before surface.

03

Furniture Procurement

Access to the trade's finest makers and artisans. Custom pieces, vintage finds, and carefully chosen antiques — each sourced with purpose.

04

Project Management

Full-scope coordination of contractors, vendors, and timelines. We manage every detail so your project moves forward without friction.

How We Work

The Process

I

Concept

We begin with conversation — learning how you live, what you love, and what a space should feel like before we consider what it should look like.

II

Curation

Material boards, sourcing, and selection — each piece chosen against the whole. We work at the pace the project requires, never rushing the good decisions.

III

Completion

Installation, styling, and the moment a space becomes yours. We stay until it's finished — and finished means something.
